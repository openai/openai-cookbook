# AUMARA Philosophy — Source Synthesis v0.1

## Purpose

This document translates the supplied philosophical and practical source materials into public-facing AUMARA language.

The source materials combine three layers:

1. poetic and metaphysical imagery;
2. practical attention and communication tools;
3. broad claims that should not be presented as universal fact.

AUMARA should use the first as metaphor, the second as practice, and handle the third with restraint.

## Public philosophy pillars

### The sphere as structure and symbol

The sphere belongs in AUMARA in two grounded ways:

- as a real geodesic structure built from triangulated geometry;
- as a metaphor for wholeness, continuity and connection.

Suggested language:

> AUMARA uses the sphere as both structure and symbol: a continuous volume, a different relationship with light, and a reminder that a place can be experienced as a whole rather than as a sequence of boxes.

### Attention before interpretation

A useful practical idea from the source material is the difference between direct sensation and the story the mind immediately builds around it.

Suggested language:

> At AUMARA, attention begins with what is physically here: the contact of the body with the chair, the sound of wind, the temperature of the air and the movement of light. This pause creates room before reaction.

### Observation before identity

Suggested language:

> The practice is not to suppress thoughts or emotions, but to create enough distance to see them clearly and respond deliberately.

### Facts before attributed intention

AUMARA communication should separate:

- what happened;
- what was interpreted;
- what is needed next.

Suggested language:

> We prefer clarity to theatre: what happened, what is needed, and what we do next.

### Contribution before performance

Suggested language:

> AUMARA programmes are designed around contribution, not display: what becomes clearer, calmer or more useful for the person, the group and the place.

## Recommended public philosophy block

### A place built for attention

AUMARA began with a simple question: what kind of place helps people become more present — not more impressed?

The answer shaped the architecture, the scale and the way programmes are hosted. The geodesic houses create continuous volume instead of conventional boxed rooms. Wood, light, air and landscape remain visible. Small groups allow privacy without isolation and community without crowding.

Our practice is equally simple: begin with what is real. Sensation before interpretation. Facts before attributed intention. Pause before reaction. Contribution before performance.

AUMARA is not a doctrine and not a promise of transformation. It is a carefully designed environment in which rest, attention, honest conversation and useful action become more possible.

## Suggested site section

### From sphere to practice

The sphere is not presented as a scientific answer. It is the architecture of the place and the organising metaphor of AUMARA: continuity instead of fragmentation, relationship instead of isolation, and enough space to see a reaction before becoming it.

The practical method is grounded and repeatable:

- notice direct sensation;
- pause the automatic story;
- distinguish observation from assumption;
- choose the next useful action;
- recognise what worked without turning it into personal theatre.

This is the philosophical bridge between the houses, the retreats and the way AUMARA hosts people.

## Editorial guardrails

The public website should not present as scientific fact:

- metaphysical claims about consciousness, vibration, topology or time;
- claims that all conflict is only self-projection;
- sweeping claims about gender, dominance or relationship hierarchy;
- psychological or therapeutic promises without evidence and qualified professionals.

The uploaded relationship transcript is not suitable as AUMARA brand copy because it relies on broad gender generalisations and hierarchy claims.

## Publishing rule

Classify each statement before publication:

- **Physical fact** — architecture, materials, capacity, location, operations. Must be verifiable.
- **Practice** — attention, reflection, communication and facilitation. Must be safe and clearly framed.
- **Metaphor** — sphere, flow, reflection and field. Must remain imagery rather than science.
- **Claim** — psychology, healing, consciousness or sustainability. Requires evidence and qualification.
