## 1. Purpose

This file is the source of truth for the first commercial AUMARA stay page. It separates verified guest-facing facts from unresolved operational details and from future retreat/event positioning.

The first release must sell the stay clearly before it introduces retreats, events or wider brand philosophy.

## 3. Do not publish until verified

- Exact land area, including “one hectare” or “nearly a hectare”.
- Unverified internal floor areas such as 45 m² or 95 m².
- Exact distance between houses.
- Absolute privacy, complete silence, no neighbours or exclusive-resort claims.
- A private pool for any individual house.
- Six bookable houses.
- Fixed or included breakfast, daily restaurant service, guaranteed paella or fixed meal prices.
- A standard early check-in promise.
- A personal automatic door code.
- Unverified universal amenity claims: parking, Wi-Fi, air conditioning, fully equipped kitchen or accessibility.
- Any therapeutic outcome or medical/psychological claim for retreat programmes.
- Old internal house names or a guessed identity for the reserve unit.

## 4. Operational facts deliberately withheld from public copy

- Check-in/check-out are not printed as a universal rule until Beds24 and guest communication agree. Current sources conflict: Beds24 shows 14:00–24:00 / 11:00; guest messages have used 16:00 / 12:00.
- A universal child-age rule is not printed until the policy is reconciled with already accepted bookings.
- Payment, cancellation and deposit rules must come from the live booking terms for the selected dates and rate.
