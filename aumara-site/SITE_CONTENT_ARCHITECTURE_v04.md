# AUMARA Website Content Architecture v0.4

## Core positioning

AUMARA is not only short-stay accommodation.

It is a nature hospitality campus with three public entry points:

1. **Stay** — private Chalet and Superior Chalet accommodation.
2. **Retreat** — hosted programmes and community-led retreats.
3. **Gather** — reviewed private, creative, family and leadership formats.

The legal operator remains EL CID VENTURES BENIDOLEIG S.L. Booking.com is an external booking/payment channel for the currently published short-stay inventory.

## Narrative spine

### 1. Arrival

A place to stay, gather and become real together.

### 2. The idea

AUMARA is a physical container for attention: private enough to rest, open enough to meet, structured enough for an online community to become a real group.

### 3. Why spheres

- continuous volume rather than conventional boxes and corridors;
- triangulated geometry;
- changing light and relationship to the landscape;
- recognisable shelter with a stronger sense of sky, trees and weather;
- beauty must support sleep, conversation, privacy, concentration and recovery.

### 4. Materials and environmental truth

- wood is the dominant visible material;
- natural light and landscape are part of the experience;
- publish only verified environmental attributes;
- never use generic eco claims as decoration;
- detailed construction, insulation, water, energy and material claims require documentation before publication.

### 5. Stay

- 6 physical houses on site;
- 5 currently bookable;
- 3 Chalet;
- 2 Superior Chalet;
- one house temporarily outside short-stay inventory.

### 6. AUMARA Retreat Campus

AUMARA is a physical setting for niche communities that already exist online and need a beautiful, safe and operationally complete place to meet.

Initial product families:

- AUMARA Women’s Reset;
- astrology / Human Design / meaning communities;
- ADHD / NeuroAdventure;
- Bring Your Own Retreat for schools and community leaders.

### 7. Safety standard

- consent-based adult space;
- no sexualised practices;
- no psychoactive substances;
- no manipulative marketing;
- no guru or cult dynamics;
- screening, GDPR, professional touch boundaries and operator right to stop unsafe programme blocks.

### 8. Private gatherings

Potential reviewed formats:

- private family weekends;
- creative residencies;
- leadership off-sites;
- community gatherings;
- small celebrations;
- hosted food experiences.

Every format is reviewed for capacity, noise, safety, accommodation, food, licensing and impact on the property.

## Content production priorities

### P0 — required for owned-domain launch

- daylight exterior hero;
- complete Chalet interior sequence;
- complete Superior Chalet interior sequence;
- pool and common areas in daylight;
- clear property map / site orientation;
- operator and contact details;
- stay / retreat / gather calls to action.

### P1 — premium emotional layer

- dawn exterior;
- sunset and blue-hour transition;
- night lighting;
- opening-the-door arrival sequence;
- food table and hosted meal;
- quiet human-scale moments;
- authentic ambient audio;
- short silent video loops.

### P2 — retreat and gathering proof

- small hosted table;
- guided walk;
- notebook / stones / hands / quiet circle;
- movement activity;
- private family or creative gathering;
- host / facilitator portraits;
- operational backstage: food, welcome, room preparation.

## Conversion rules

- booking availability remains visible;
- every emotional section leads to Stay, Retreat or Gather;
- Booking.com is clearly identified as external;
- private-retreat and event leads go directly to the operator;
- no environmental, therapeutic or programme claim is published without operational proof.
