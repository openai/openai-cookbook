The idea

AUMARA is not simply a place to sleep. It is a physical container for attention: private enough to rest, open enough to meet, and structured enough for an online community to become a real group.

Why spheres

Architecture without the feeling of a box.

The geodesic form changes the way a room is felt. Triangulated geometry creates a continuous volume, light moves differently across the interior, and the house reads as one space rather than a sequence of conventional walls and corridors.

We chose the sphere as a meeting point between structure and landscape: a recognisable human shelter that still feels connected to sky, trees and weather.

Human scaleSmall groups, complete houses and direct hosting instead of anonymous mass tourism.
Natural presenceWood is the dominant visible material; light, air, landscape and quiet are treated as part of the accommodation.
Truth before eco-languageWe publish only environmental characteristics that are physically present and documented. Sustainability is not used as decoration.
Beauty with a functionThe architecture must support sleep, conversation, privacy, concentration and recovery — not merely photographs.
