What AUMARA is

Not a hotel room. Not a generic villa.

AUMARA brings together the privacy of a complete house with the support of a hosted stay. The houses sit inside the wider EL CID ecosystem: outdoor space, restaurant access, practical hospitality, family stays and retreat potential.
