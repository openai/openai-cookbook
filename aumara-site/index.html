How booking works

The site presents AUMARA. Booking.com closes the transaction.

Current rates, restrictions, availability, confirmation and payment stay inside Booking.com until the PMS/channel connection is fully authorised and tested.

01Choose dates here

02Confirm on Booking.com

03Receive arrival instructions

Check-in and access information are sent before arrival through the confirmed operational channel.
