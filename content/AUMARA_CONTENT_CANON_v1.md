# AUMARA — Content Canon v1

Status: canonical working source for website, Ads, Booking/Beds24, social media and presentations.

## Approved hero — use verbatim

> **Hay lugares que te dan más.**  
> **En AUMARA, vuelves a encontrarte contigo mismo.**

Working English translation:

> **There are places that give you more.**  
> **At AUMARA, you find your way back to yourself.**

The Spanish wording is approved. Do not replace it with a new interpretation without explicit approval.

## Positioning

AUMARA is not primarily sold as a list of amenities or as “six houses.” The emotional product is returning to yourself through private space, landscape, light, quiet and deliberate choices.

Preferred direction:

- reconnecting rather than generic “disconnecting”;
- choosing better rather than consuming more;
- a complete private house with a human hospitality layer;
- real Marina Alta landscape;
- quiet confidence rather than luxury clichés;
- atmosphere before explanation.

## Visual principle

> **La atmósfera vende más que el discurso.**

Use real material:

- morning light on wood;
- coffee on a terrace;
- house exteriors;
- paths between houses;
- leaves moving in the wind;
- panoramic valley and mountain views;
- real walkthrough video.

Avoid generic wellness stock photography, dark heavy layouts and catalogue-first grids.

## Product boundary

AUMARA is a separate brand at `elcidspain.com/aumara/`.

It is not an EL CID hotel room category. It has its own:

- official logo and brandbook;
- photographs and video;
- Chalet and Superior Chalet inventory;
- walkthrough;
- Beds24 booking journey;
- emotional language.

EL CID Country Club remains the root site at `elcidspain.com/` and covers hotel rooms, studio, restaurant, pool, terraces, sport and events.

## Walkthrough

The walkthrough is a core commercial proof, not decoration:

- real plan;
- eight real points;
- short MP4 clips;
- movement point to point;
- clear spatial understanding before booking;
- direct Beds24 and WhatsApp access.

Approved direction:

> **Antes de llegar, empieza a recorrerlo.**

## Tone of voice

- adult and emotionally intelligent;
- short, native Spanish phrases;
- sensory and specific;
- warm but not sugary;
- reflective but not therapeutic;
- premium through restraint;
- no guru language;
- no generic “luxury escape” wording.

## Do not use

- “small hotel” positioning;
- AUMARA as an EL CID room category;
- inventory counts as the primary hero;
- generic `disconnect` language;
- therapy, healing or transformation claims;
- absolute privacy without proof;
- exact distances or cadastral size before verification;
- `glamping` as a legal category without registration support;
- invented slogans that overwrite approved copy.
