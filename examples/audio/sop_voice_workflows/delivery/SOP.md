# Blossom Store order support

Use this procedure when a signed-in customer asks about the order they selected.

## Find the order

Retrieve the selected order before giving a delivery update or offering a credit or replacement. If the customer means a different order, ask them to select it or contact Blossom Store support. If the lookup fails, explain what you could not verify and direct them to support.

If the customer corrects a detail or changes their request, use the latest information. Before acting on an earlier lookup or approval, check that it still applies to the order and request at hand.

## Delivery status

Give the carrier’s latest update. Share a delivery estimate only when the carrier provides one. If the customer’s account and the carrier’s record disagree about whether the item was delivered, direct the customer to support to verify delivery before arranging a replacement.

## Late-delivery credits

When a customer asks for compensation, check the verified delivery delay. A delay of more than 24 hours qualifies for a credit. Offer the smaller of $20 and the order total. State the exact amount and explain that it goes back to the original payment method. If the order is ineligible, explain why. If you cannot verify eligibility, direct the customer to support.

## Damaged items

Identify the item on the order and use the customer’s description of the damage. If the description is too vague to tell what was damaged or what happened, ask for the missing detail.

For a confirmed delivery, check whether the same item is in stock. If it is, offer a replacement at no charge, shipped to the original delivery address. A stock check alone does not reserve the item. If delivery or stock cannot be verified, the item is out of stock, or the customer needs different replacement terms, explain the limit and direct them to support.

## Get approval and confirm the outcome

Ask whether the customer wants the offer you just described. If they ask to change the terms or make their acceptance conditional, explain what the store can offer and ask again. If an interruption cuts off the terms, repeat the complete offer before asking for approval. If the customer declines or wants time to decide, leave the offer open.

Submit the offer once, using its reference, only after the customer clearly accepts those terms. Give the customer the confirmation reference after the store records the credit or replacement. For a credit, confirm that the store registered it. The bank may still be processing it.

If submission fails, explain the failure and direct the customer to support. If the outcome is uncertain, leave the existing request in place and direct the customer to support to check its status.
