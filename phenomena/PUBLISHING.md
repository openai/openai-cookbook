# PHENOMENA — Publishing Strategy

## Editorial home

Primary home: Substack publication with Russian and English sections.

Archive: GitHub `phenomena/` and later a simple branded site or domain.

## Distribution roles

Threads: hooks, fragments, extended text attachments and conversation.

LinkedIn: English founder essays, selected Russian pieces, native long-form and author video.

Instagram: author-avatar video, carousel excerpts and audio fragments.

YouTube: narrated visual essays of two to five minutes.

Telegram: Russian direct-reader channel and discussion.

## Repeatable package for every essay

1. Canonical Russian essay.
2. Canonical English literary adaptation.
3. Threads entry and extended text version.
4. LinkedIn native adaptation.
5. Sixty-to-one-hundred-and-eighty-second author-video script.
6. Quote cards or carousel fragments.
7. Audio version.

## First release

001 — «Комиссия по качеству мироздания / The Committee for the Quality of Reality».

## Launch actions

- Create the Substack publication and reserve the project name.
- Prepare the author biography and publication description in Russian and English.
- Publish essay 001 in both languages.
- Produce one HeyGen author video from the strongest ninety-second section.
- Release fragments across Threads, LinkedIn and Instagram over seven to ten days.
- Track saves, replies, subscriptions and completion signals rather than raw views alone.
